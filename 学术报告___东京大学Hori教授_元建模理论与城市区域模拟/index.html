讲座简介
本研究基于元建模理论，建立并强化了结构力学与连续体介质力学之间的联系。该理论显示，基于结构力学的模型与其连续体介质模型具有较好的一贯性，因为这两种模型具有相同的力学特性和结构特性。本讨论会首先将介绍元建模理论，并以几个城市区域为案例，展示其在特定地震场景下地震灾害评估中的应用。
A meta-modeling theory has been developed to strengthen the link between structure mechanics and continuum mechanics. According to this theory, it is shown that certain models based on structure mechanics are consistent with a model based on continuum mechanics in the sense that they share the identical mechanical and structural characteristics. The seminar presents the meta-modeling theory and provides some examples of urban area models which are used for earthquake hazard and disaster estimation for a given earthquake scenario.
